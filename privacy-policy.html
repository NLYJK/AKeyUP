Privacy Policy

Simple information about your data, browser storage, advertisements, and privacy.

1. Third-Party Services

AKeyUP uses third-party services and advertising providers, including Adsterra. These services may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies to deliver advertisements, measure performance, prevent fraud, and improve their services.

AKeyUP does not control how these third parties collect, store, or use information. Any data collected by third-party services is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

2. Browser Storage and Local Data

Some AKeyUP tools store information directly inside your browser using technologies such as localStorage and IndexedDB.

This data remains on your device and is used to provide tool functionality, remember settings, and save information between visits.

Examples include:

  • Notes
  • Word Counter preferences
  • Theme settings
  • Other browser-based tool data

You can remove this data at any time through your browser settings.

3. Local-Only Tools

Many AKeyUP tools are designed to work entirely within your browser.

For tools such as Notes, text and content are stored locally on your device and are not intentionally transmitted to or stored on AKeyUP servers during normal use.

Because the information remains on your device, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and browser.

4. Advertising

Advertisements displayed on AKeyUP may be provided by third-party advertising networks.

These advertising providers may collect information through cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies in accordance with their own privacy practices.

AKeyUP does not sell your personal notes, text, or locally stored tool data.
