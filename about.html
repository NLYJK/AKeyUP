What is AKeyUP?

A simple tool to open websites quickly.
Helps save time for people who use multiple websites daily.
Runs locally on your computer for better privacy.

Why AKeyUP was made?

It was created because repeatedly reopening websites after accidentally closing them was frustrating and time-consuming.
