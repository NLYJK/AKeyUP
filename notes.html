📖 About AKeyUP Notes

AKeyUP Notes helps you manage daily notes, ideas, reminders, tasks, and quick information without needing an account. Everything is stored locally inside your browser, helping you keep your notes private while still giving you fast access anytime. The notes tool includes pinning, search, note colors, tags, export support, auto-saving drafts, and a responsive design for both mobile and desktop devices.
